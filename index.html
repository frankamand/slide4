Rules, voice and stats

Tap a tile next to the gap to slide it in (arrow keys work too). Four of one colour in a row or column scores 1,000, five scores 3,000, and two lines crossing score double. Every colour above six adds 25%. Instead of a tenth colour, the board grows to 6×6 with eight colours and climbs again to thirteen; the multiplier keeps rising (×3.25 at the top) and a line of six scores 6,000. Matched tiles get new colours that never form another line. If no colour has 4 tiles left, the board turns orange and lines of 3 score 500 (and 3 seconds in timed mode) until some colour has 4 again.

Timed: you start with 6 colours and a one-minute bar that runs down. A line of 4 adds 5 seconds, a line of 5 adds 15. Every two minutes another colour joins, up to 10. The game ends when the bar runs out.
